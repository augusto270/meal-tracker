Portions without a scale

  • Palm100 g cooked meat, fish or tofu. About a deck of cards.
  • Fist1 cup of rice, pasta, cereal, fruit or vegetables.
  • Cupped hand½ cup, or a small handful of nuts or chips (about 1 oz).
  • Whole thumb1 tablespoon of oil, butter, peanut butter, mayo or dressing.
  • Thumb tip1 teaspoon of sugar or oil.
  • Two thumbs1 oz of cheese.

Better estimates

  • Count cooking oil. 1 tbsp is about 120 kcal and it is the most-missed item. Add it on its own.
  • Packaged food: the label is exact. Use Quick add with the kcal per serving.
  • Eating out: many chains post calories. Otherwise pick the closest food and round up.
  • Round up 10–20% when unsure. Almost everyone underestimates portions.
  • Calibrate once: serve with measuring cups for a week so you learn what 1 cup looks like on your plate.
  • Trust the trend: if your 7-day weight average isn't moving as planned, adjust your target.
